# Permission requests (drafts — send from your own address)

Each email is short, says what we use, how it is shown, and that the project is free and non-commercial.
Track replies in the table at the end and update `ingest/sources.yaml` → `status`.

**Project blurb (reused below)**
> QuranGraph is a free, non-commercial website that helps beginners understand the Quran in English and Bangla.
> It links every ayah to its words, roots, themes, people, places and stories, and shows recognised translations
> and tafsir exactly as published, with full credit. It has no ads and no accounts, and it starts as a private
> site for scholarly review before any public launch.
